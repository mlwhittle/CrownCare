Your Client’s Hair Journey Does Not Stop When She Leaves Your Chair

CrownCare is a between-appointment hair journey platform for hairstylists and their clients. It helps stylists track client progress photos, hair routines, product use, treatment notes, scalp and hair concerns, and client updates between salon visits.

Most salon apps help manage bookings, payments, calendars, and appointments. CrownCare begins where those apps stop — after the client leaves the chair.

With CrownCare, hairstylists can better understand what happens between appointments, prepare for the next visit, support better client care, and strengthen client retention.
