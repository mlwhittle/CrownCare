Asymmetry in the follicle shape—specifically asymmetry in the dermal papilla—creates curl.Cellular Force: An asymmetrical arrangement of dividing cells and structural proteins within the follicle creates an "uneven cellular force." This force physically drives the hair fiber into a non-linear, coiled shape as it emerges.Retro-Curved Follicles: Unique to tightly coily/Type 4 hair, these follicles curve in two directions simultaneously (left-to-right and fore-to-back). This produces a zig-zag or corkscrew pattern.Hydration Link: This corkscrew pattern makes Type 4 hair inherently drier, as scalp oils cannot easily travel down the complex, non-linear path of the hair shaft compared to straighter fibers.Texture GeneticsBlack African Hair: Variation in curl is a "complex" genetic trait governed by multiple genes acting in concert.Western European Hair: Straightness is frequently linked to specific genetic polymorphisms affecting trichohyalin, a critical inner root sheath protein.--------------------------------------------------------------------------------3. Porosity and Hydration MethodologyLow Porosity Care: The LOC MethodLow porosity hair features tightly packed cuticles that resist hydration. The system prioritizes immediate sealing to trap moisture.Liquid: Apply water or a curl-defining moisturizer to damp hair.Oil: Smooth a sealing oil to create an immediate moisture barrier.Cream: Finish with a moisturizing cream/butter to soften and define.Intelligence Protocol:Seek: Glycerin and honey.Essential: Use protein-free conditioners; proteins often cause surface buildup rather than absorbing.Avoid: Heavy oils that sit atop the cuticle.High Porosity Care: The LCO MethodHigh porosity hair absorbs moisture rapidly but loses it through "flash evaporation." This requires a rich internal barrier before the final seal.Liquid: Apply water or a liquid hydrator.Cream: Apply a rich cream or butter to smooth the cuticle and wrap the strands in deep nourishment.Oil: Apply oil as the final top layer to lock in the liquid and cream layers.General Porosity DoctrineCrownCare leverages AI to map specific porosity profiles against routine logs. By using pattern recognition, the system determines exactly when a user's hair responds best to hydration vs. protein, allowing the AI to predict and prevent breakage before the hair fiber fails.--------------------------------------------------------------------------------4.
