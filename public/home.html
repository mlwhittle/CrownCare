Your hair journey, organized.

CrownCare is a hair growth and hair care tracking app designed to help users document their progress over time. Instead of guessing what worked, users can track photos, treatments, routines, notes, and progress patterns in one place.

  • ✓ Save visual progress photos in a private hair diary
  • ✓ Track routines, treatments, and care habits
  • ✓ Review progress reports over time
  • ✓ Keep notes about products, styles, and scalp concerns
  • ✓ Connect with a stylist when professional guidance is needed

Built for people serious about hair care consistency.

CrownCare is for anyone who wants a clearer way to understand their hair journey. It is especially useful for people tracking growth, thinning, shedding, protective styles, scalp changes, routine consistency, or recovery after hair setbacks.
